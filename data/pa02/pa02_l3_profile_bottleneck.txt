## E. PA02 target selection (experiment-based)

Three targets are **not** chosen by reading loops; they map to distinct matcher stages with measured cost in this bag run.

### Excluded from PA02 optimization

- **score_all** (34,785.6 ms): PA01 scope — fixed L9 + GPU threshold=256
- **MakeScans/MakeBounds/ToOut**: not separately tagged; remainder after tagged costs

### Selected targets (ranked by experimental relevance)

| rank | target | Phase | cumulative (ms) | % match | experimental basis |
|-----:|--------|------:|----------------:|--------:|--------------------|
| 1 | `Branch` | 2 | 44,796.3 | 51.0% | Branch cumulative=44,796.3 ms (match의 51.0%). depth stratum으로 coarse(depth=3) 집중… |
| 2 | `Score` | 3 | 57,074.7 | 65.0% | Score_orchestration=22,235.1 ms (Score cumulative의 39.0%, match의 25.3%). n_cand … |
| 3 | `make_cand` | 1 | 820.0 | 0.9% | match의 0.9%로 절대 비중은 작으나, 호출 34,665회·격리 microbench 가능·side effect 적음 → Phase 1 검증… |

### Target: `make_cand` (Phase 1)

- **Role:** 후보 격자 (cx,cy) 생성 — B&B coarse 탐색 공간
- **Measured:** cumulative=820.0 ms, calls=34,740, 0.9% of match
- **Selection:** match의 0.9%로 절대 비중은 작으나, 호출 34,665회·격리 microbench 가능·side effect 적음 → Phase 1 검증 타깃
- **Hot strata:**
  - grid_span=16x16: 34,528 calls, 814.9 ms elapsed sum
  - grid_span=16: 6 calls, 0.1 ms elapsed sum
  - grid_span=16x: 4 calls, 0.1 ms elapsed sum

### Target: `Score` (Phase 3)

- **Role:** 스캔별 score_all 호출 + sort — B&B 점수 평가 오케스트레이션
- **Measured:** cumulative=57,074.7 ms, calls=80,428, 65.0% of match
- **Selection:** Score_orchestration=22,235.1 ms (Score cumulative의 39.0%, match의 25.3%). n_cand stratum에서 coarse(3840) vs leaf(4) 이중 regime → Phase 3 실험 대상
- **Hot strata:**
  - n_cand=3840: 2,308 calls, 40,521.6 ms elapsed sum
  - n_cand=4: 75,507 calls, 15,924.2 ms elapsed sum
  - n_cand=2: 2,384 calls, 443.3 ms elapsed sum

### Target: `Branch` (Phase 2)

- **Role:** Branch-and-Bound 재귀·가지치기·child split (내부 Score 포함)
- **Measured:** cumulative=44,796.3 ms, calls=80,428, 51.0% of match
- **Selection:** Branch cumulative=44,796.3 ms (match의 51.0%). depth stratum으로 coarse(depth=3) 집중 확인 → Phase 2 대상
- **Hot strata:**
  - depth=3: 2,311 calls, 32,448.0 ms elapsed sum
  - depth=2: 54,867 calls, 9,603.4 ms elapsed sum
  - depth=1: 16,712 calls, 2,656.9 ms elapsed sum
  - depth=0: 6,330 calls, 0.0 ms elapsed sum
