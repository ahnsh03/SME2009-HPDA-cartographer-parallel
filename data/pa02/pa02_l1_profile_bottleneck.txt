## E. PA02 target selection (experiment-based)

Three targets are **not** chosen by reading loops; they map to distinct matcher stages with measured cost in this bag run.

### Excluded from PA02 optimization

- **score_all** (34,596.6 ms): PA01 scope — fixed L9 + GPU threshold=256
- **MakeScans/MakeBounds/ToOut**: not separately tagged; remainder after tagged costs

### Selected targets (ranked by experimental relevance)

| rank | target | Phase | cumulative (ms) | % match | experimental basis |
|-----:|--------|------:|----------------:|--------:|--------------------|
| 1 | `Branch` | 2 | 53,773.1 | 59.3% | Branch cumulative=53,773.1 ms (match의 59.3%). depth stratum으로 coarse(depth=3) 집중… |
| 2 | `Score` | 3 | 60,230.2 | 66.4% | Score_orchestration=25,653.4 ms (Score cumulative의 42.6%, match의 28.3%). n_cand … |
| 3 | `make_cand` | 1 | 763.2 | 0.8% | match의 0.8%로 절대 비중은 작으나, 호출 32,444회·격리 microbench 가능·side effect 적음 → Phase 1 검증… |

### Target: `make_cand` (Phase 1)

- **Role:** 후보 격자 (cx,cy) 생성 — B&B coarse 탐색 공간
- **Measured:** cumulative=763.2 ms, calls=32,505, 0.8% of match
- **Selection:** match의 0.8%로 절대 비중은 작으나, 호출 32,444회·격리 microbench 가능·side effect 적음 → Phase 1 검증 타깃
- **Hot strata:**
  - grid_span=16x16: 32,325 calls, 758.9 ms elapsed sum
  - grid_span=16: 4 calls, 0.1 ms elapsed sum
  - grid_span=16x: 3 calls, 0.1 ms elapsed sum

### Target: `Score` (Phase 3)

- **Role:** 스캔별 score_all 호출 + sort — B&B 점수 평가 오케스트레이션
- **Measured:** cumulative=60,230.2 ms, calls=80,383, 66.4% of match
- **Selection:** Score_orchestration=25,653.4 ms (Score cumulative의 42.6%, match의 28.3%). n_cand stratum에서 coarse(3840) vs leaf(4) 이중 regime → Phase 3 실험 대상
- **Hot strata:**
  - n_cand=3840: 2,163 calls, 42,379.6 ms elapsed sum
  - n_cand=4: 76,093 calls, 17,348.1 ms elapsed sum
  - n_cand=2: 1,937 calls, 386.0 ms elapsed sum

### Target: `Branch` (Phase 2)

- **Role:** Branch-and-Bound 재귀·가지치기·child split (내부 Score 포함)
- **Measured:** cumulative=53,773.1 ms, calls=80,383, 59.3% of match
- **Selection:** Branch cumulative=53,773.1 ms (match의 59.3%). depth stratum으로 coarse(depth=3) 집중 확인 → Phase 2 대상
- **Hot strata:**
  - depth=3: 2,159 calls, 34,040.8 ms elapsed sum
  - depth=2: 45,051 calls, 14,388.9 ms elapsed sum
  - depth=1: 21,122 calls, 5,180.2 ms elapsed sum
  - depth=0: 11,813 calls, 0.1 ms elapsed sum

## F. Implementation order (not impact order)

| Phase | target | why this order |
|------:|--------|----------------|
| 1 | make_cand | smallest side effects; validates experiment pipeline |
| 2 | Branch | depth=3 stratum largest B&B cost; after Phase 1 regression check |
| 3 | Score | Score_orchestration + dual n_cand regime; CPU/GPU choice needs sweep |
