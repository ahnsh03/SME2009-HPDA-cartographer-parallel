## E. PA02 target selection (experiment-based)

Three targets are **not** chosen by reading loops; they map to distinct matcher stages with measured cost in this bag run.

### Excluded from PA02 optimization

- **score_all** (33,814.3 ms): PA01 scope — fixed L9 + GPU threshold=256
- **MakeScans/MakeBounds/ToOut**: not separately tagged; remainder after tagged costs

### Selected targets (ranked by experimental relevance)

| rank | target | Phase | cumulative (ms) | % match | experimental basis |
|-----:|--------|------:|----------------:|--------:|--------------------|
| 1 | `Branch` | 2 | 54,743.0 | 60.5% | Branch cumulative=54,743.0 ms (match의 60.5%). depth stratum으로 coarse(depth=3) 집중… |
| 2 | `Score` | 3 | 59,868.5 | 66.2% | Score_orchestration=25,954.9 ms (Score cumulative의 43.4%, match의 28.7%). n_cand … |
| 3 | `make_cand` | 1 | 1,215.7 | 1.3% | match의 1.3%로 절대 비중은 작으나, 호출 31,705회·격리 microbench 가능·side effect 적음 → Phase 1 검증… |

### Target: `make_cand` (Phase 1)

- **Role:** 후보 격자 (cx,cy) 생성 — B&B coarse 탐색 공간
- **Measured:** cumulative=1,215.7 ms, calls=31,755, 1.3% of match
- **Selection:** match의 1.3%로 절대 비중은 작으나, 호출 31,705회·격리 microbench 가능·side effect 적음 → Phase 1 검증 타깃
- **Hot strata:**
  - grid_span=16x16: 31,599 calls, 1,209.6 ms elapsed sum
  - grid_span=16: 2 calls, 0.1 ms elapsed sum
  - grid_span=16x: 1 calls, 0.0 ms elapsed sum

### Target: `Score` (Phase 3)

- **Role:** 스캔별 score_all 호출 + sort — B&B 점수 평가 오케스트레이션
- **Measured:** cumulative=59,868.5 ms, calls=78,275, 66.2% of match
- **Selection:** Score_orchestration=25,954.9 ms (Score cumulative의 43.4%, match의 28.7%). n_cand stratum에서 coarse(3840) vs leaf(4) 이중 regime → Phase 3 실험 대상
- **Hot strata:**
  - n_cand=3840: 2,110 calls, 41,445.4 ms elapsed sum
  - n_cand=4: 74,147 calls, 17,836.4 ms elapsed sum
  - n_cand=2: 1,788 calls, 390.4 ms elapsed sum

### Target: `Branch` (Phase 2)

- **Role:** Branch-and-Bound 재귀·가지치기·child split (내부 Score 포함)
- **Measured:** cumulative=54,743.0 ms, calls=78,275, 60.5% of match
- **Selection:** Branch cumulative=54,743.0 ms (match의 60.5%). depth stratum으로 coarse(depth=3) 집중 확인 → Phase 2 대상
- **Hot strata:**
  - depth=3: 2,113 calls, 34,679.3 ms elapsed sum
  - depth=2: 43,690 calls, 14,673.0 ms elapsed sum
  - depth=1: 20,653 calls, 5,258.0 ms elapsed sum
  - depth=0: 11,592 calls, 0.3 ms elapsed sum
